Srinivasa Ramanujan Centre (SRC)

Srinivasa Ramanujan Centre is SASTRA's off-campus centre at Kumbakonam.

SRC is named as a tribute to the great mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan, who spent most of his formative years in Kumbakonam.

SRC was inaugurated in November 2000 by Prof. V. S. Ramamurthy, Secretary, Department of Science and Technology, Government of India.

The centre was established to provide quality higher education to the people of Kumbakonam and nearby places.

SRC is an off-campus centre under SASTRA Deemed University.

The centre has modern classrooms, a computer laboratory, engineering and science laboratories, an air-conditioned auditorium and a library.

SRC also has a museum related to Srinivasa Ramanujan's life and work.

On 20 December 2003, Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam dedicated SRC and the house in which Ramanujan lived in Kumbakonam to the nation.

SRC also has the Srinivasa Ramanujan House of Mathematics, a museum on Srinivasa Ramanujan.
